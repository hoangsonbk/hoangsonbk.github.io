Privacy Policy

cowork-studio · Last updated 30 October 2026

cowork-studio is a personal publishing tool run by the owner of the TamaArt and Little Home pages. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner. It runs only on the owner's own computer.

What the app does

  • Publishes posts and short videos the owner made to the owner's own Facebook Pages, Instagram account and YouTube channel.
  • Reads comments left on the owner's Facebook Page posts so the owner can draft and send replies as the Page.

What data it accesses

  • Access tokens for the owner's Facebook Pages and linked Instagram account, and a Google authorization for the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Public comments on the Pages' own posts: the comment text, the commenter's name, the time and the comment id.
  • From YouTube, only what is needed to upload the owner's videos and to confirm which channel they go to (the channel's name and id).

How the data is used and stored

  • Comment text is used only to draft a reply for the owner to approve. A draft may be written by an AI model, and the comment and draft are sent to the owner's private Slack channel for approval.
  • All data is stored in a local database on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

To have a comment or any other data about you removed from the app's storage, contact the Page through Facebook Messenger, or email hoangsonjpn@gmail.com. The data is deleted within 30 days of the request. The owner can revoke the app's YouTube access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
